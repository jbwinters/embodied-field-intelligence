1. see the POMDP structure;
2. see the exact state update equations;
3. analyze the semigroup generated by the (local) update operators; and

## 0) Core objects and notation

* Let $H,W\in\mathbb{N}$ be grid height/width.
  The grid is the vertex set $V=\{0,\dots,H-1\}\times\{0,\dots,W-1\}$ with 4‑neighborhood

  $$
  \mathcal{N}(y,x)=\{(y\!\pm\!1,x),(y,x\!\pm\!1)\}\cap V .
  $$

* An **episode** has fixed parameters

  $$
  \Theta_{\text{env}}=(H,W,\;p_{\text{wall}},\;n_A,n_B,\;T_{\max},\;r_{\text{step}},\;r_{\text{bump}},\;r_A,\;r_B),
  $$

  and fixed **agent/config** parameters $\Theta_{\text{agent}}$ given later.

* Arrays are indexed by $V$. For any Boolean mask $M$, write $\mathbf{1}_M$ for its indicator array.

* Walls are **hard obstacles**; the agent occupies one cell $(y_t,x_t)\in V$.

## 1) Environment $\mathcal{E}$ (ForageWorld)

### 1.1 State, initialization, and termination

An environment state at time $t$ is

$$
s_t=(y_t,x_t,\;W,\;T^A_t,\;T^B_t,\;t)
$$

where $W\subset V$ are walls, $T^A_t,T^B_t\subset V$ are locations of remaining A/B targets.

**Episode initialization.**

* Sample $W$ by $\mathbb{P}[(y,x)\in W]=p_{\text{wall}}$ i.i.d., then force the outer border to be free.
* Sample without replacement $n_A+n_B$ free cells for targets; first $n_A$ become $T^A_0$, next $n_B$ become $T^B_0$.
* Sample a free start cell $(y_0,x_0)\notin W\cup T^A_0\cup T^B_0$.
* Set $t=0$.

**Termination:** at step $t$,

$$
\text{done}_t \;\Leftrightarrow\; \big(t\ge T_{\max}\big)\;\;\text{or}\;\;\big(T^A_t=\emptyset\;\wedge\;T^B_t=\emptyset\big).
$$

### 1.2 Observation

Fix an odd observation window $\text{win}\in\mathbb{N}$. Define the window half‑size $h=\lfloor \text{win}/2\rfloor$ and the local patch

$$
\mathcal{W}_t = \{(y_t+\Delta y,\,x_t+\Delta x):\; |\Delta y|\le h,\ |\Delta x|\le h\}\cap V.
$$

The observation is four binary channels flattened to $\mathbb{R}^{4\,\text{win}^2}$:

$$
o_t = \big[\; \mathbf{1}_W,\ \mathbf{1}_{T^A_t},\ \mathbf{1}_{T^B_t},\ \mathbf{1}_{\{(y_t,x_t)\}}\;\big]_{\mathcal{W}_t}.
$$

Thus the environment with observation is a **POMDP**.

### 1.3 Transition and reward

Given action $a_t\in\{0,1,2,3\}$ (up,down,left,right) with displacement $d(a_t)\in\{(-1,0),(1,0),(0,-1),(0,1)\}$, propose

$$
(\tilde y,\tilde x)=(y_t,x_t)+d(a_t).
$$

If $(\tilde y,\tilde x)\in V\setminus W$, then the agent **moves** to that cell; otherwise it remains and incurs a bump penalty.

Let

$$
r_t = r_{\text{step}} \;+\; r_{\text{bump}}\cdot\mathbf{1}\big[(\tilde y,\tilde x)\in W\big] \;+\;
r_A\cdot\mathbf{1}\big[(y_{t+1},x_{t+1})\in T^A_t\big] \;+\;
r_B\cdot\mathbf{1}\big[(y_{t+1},x_{t+1})\in T^B_t\big].
$$

If a target is collected, it is removed:

$$
T^A_{t+1}=T^A_t\setminus\{(y_{t+1},x_{t+1})\},\qquad
T^B_{t+1}=T^B_t\setminus\{(y_{t+1},x_{t+1})\}.
$$

## 2) Agent internal state and “fast fields”

At each step the agent maintains arrays (all over $V$):

* **Attractors:** $G^A_t,G^B_t,N_t\in[0,\infty)$ (A/B “scents”, Novelty)
* **Repulsor:** $V_t\in[0,\infty)$ (visit‑trail)
* **Frontier helper:** $U_t\in[0,1]$ (unseen/frontier field, not persistent)
* **Known walls:** $K_t\subseteq V$ (monotone nondecreasing)
* **Seen cells:** $S_t\subseteq V$ (monotone nondecreasing)

It also keeps scalars/vectors:

* **Valence weights** $w^A_t,w^B_t\in[-C,C]$ (learned), and $w^{\text{nov}}$ (fixed unless you decide to learn it).
* **Repulsor weights** $k^{\text{trail}},k^{\text{corner}}>0$ (from config).
* **Bookkeeping:** last action $a_{t-1}$, stuck counter, previous local potential $P^{\text{loc}}_{t-1}$, previous patch $\pi_{t-1}$.

Ablation flags simply zero out the corresponding channels or terms.

## 3) Wall‑aware diffusion operator

Define the **masked diffusion** operator parameterized by a wall mask $M\subseteq V$, a diffusion rate $\delta\in[0,1]$, and a multiplicative decay $\lambda\in[0,1]$:

$$
\boxed{\;
\mathsf{D}_{M}^{\delta,\lambda}(F)
=
(1-\lambda)\Big[(1-\delta)F \;+\; \delta\,\mathsf{A}_M(F)\Big],\quad
\text{with}\; (\mathsf{D}_{M}^{\delta,\lambda}(F))\big|_{M}=0\;,
}
$$

where $\mathsf{A}_M$ averages 4‑neighbors **restricted to passable neighbors**,

$$
(\mathsf{A}_M(F))(y,x)=
\frac{\displaystyle\sum_{(y',x')\in\mathcal{N}(y,x)} F(y',x')\,\mathbf{1}_{V\setminus M}(y',x')}
{\displaystyle\sum_{(y',x')\in\mathcal{N}(y,x)} \mathbf{1}_{V\setminus M}(y',x')}
\;\;(\text{with }0/0:=0).
$$

This enforces zero value on walls and **no flux across walls** (as in the code).

## 4) Fast‑field updates per step

All updates below are the ones actually implemented (order matches the agent’s `step` methods and the runner).

### 4.1 Seeding and diffusion of A/B “scents”

From the observation patch, define instantaneous seeds

$$
\mathcal{S}^A_t=\{v\in\mathcal{W}_t:\text{A‑channel}=1\},\qquad
\mathcal{S}^B_t=\{v\in\mathcal{W}_t:\text{B‑channel}=1\},
$$

and newly discovered walls

$$
K_{t}^{\text{new}}=\{v\in\mathcal{W}_t:\text{wall‑channel}=1\}.
$$

Update knowledge: $K_t\leftarrow K_{t-1}\cup K^{\text{new}}_t$.
Mark seen: $S_t \leftarrow S_{t-1}\cup \mathcal{W}_t$.

Seeding is a “max‑inject” with strength $\sigma>0$:

$$
G^A_t(v)\leftarrow \max\big(G^A_t(v),\sigma\big)\ \forall v\in\mathcal{S}^A_t,\quad
G^B_t(v)\leftarrow \max\big(G^B_t(v),\sigma\big)\ \forall v\in\mathcal{S}^B_t.
$$

Then diffuse both with known‑wall mask $K_t$:

$$
G^A_t \leftarrow (\mathsf{D}_{K_t}^{\delta_{\text{scent}},\lambda_{\text{scent}}})^{s_{\text{A}}}(G^A_t),\qquad
G^B_t \leftarrow (\mathsf{D}_{K_t}^{\delta_{\text{scent}},\lambda_{\text{scent}}})^{s_{\text{B}}}(G^B_t),
$$

with $s_{\text{A}}=s_{\text{B}}=\text{scent\_steps}$.

### 4.2 Trail $V_t$ (repulsor, with cap and ping‑pong boost)

Let $p_t\in\{0,1\}$ detect “ping‑pong” (position at $t-2$ equals $t$, different from $t-1$). Effective injection
$\nu_{\text{inj}}=\text{v\_inj}\cdot(2.5 \text{ if }p_t=1 \text{ else }1)$.

Update:

$$
\begin{aligned}
&V_t(y_t,x_t)\leftarrow \min\big(V_t(y_t,x_t)+\nu_{\text{inj}},\;v_{\max}\big),\\
&V_t \leftarrow (1-\nu_{\text{decay}})V_t,\\
&V_t \leftarrow \mathsf{D}_{K_t}^{\delta_{\text{trail}},\;\tilde\lambda}(V_t)\quad(\tilde\lambda\text{ is a small diffusion‑time decay}).
\end{aligned}
$$

### 4.3 Novelty $N_t$ (attractor, clamped to $[0,1]$)

Let the **local fast potential** be $P^{\text{loc}}_t:=G^A_t(y_t,x_t)+G^B_t(y_t,x_t)$.
Define

$$
d_{\text{scent}}:=\big|P^{\text{loc}}_{t}-P^{\text{loc}}_{t-1}\big|,\qquad
d_{\text{obs}}:=\frac{1}{3\,\text{win}^2}\sum_{\text{walls,A,B channels}} \big|\pi_t-\pi_{t-1}\big|,
$$

where $\pi_t$ is the 3‑channel patch (excluding the agent channel).

Prediction error scalar:

$$
\varepsilon_t := \tfrac12 d_{\text{scent}}+\tfrac12 d_{\text{obs}}.
$$

Update (gain $g>0$, decay $\eta_N$, diffusion $\delta_N$, small $\tilde\lambda$):

$$
\begin{aligned}
&N_t \leftarrow (1-\eta_N)N_t,\\
&N_t(y_t,x_t)\leftarrow \min\big(1,\;\max\big(N_t(y_t,x_t),\; g\,\varepsilon_t\big)\big),\\
&N_t \leftarrow \mathsf{D}_{K_t}^{\delta_N,\;\tilde\lambda}(N_t).
\end{aligned}
$$

### 4.4 Frontier $U_t$ (helper attractor built on seen/known)

Let $\overline S_t=V\setminus S_t$ (unseen) and $\overline K_t=V\setminus K_t$ (not known wall). Define the raw frontier mask

$$
U^{\text{raw}}_t := \mathbf{1}_{\overline S_t}\cdot \mathbf{1}_{\overline K_t}.
$$

Diffuse three times with $\delta_U,\lambda_U$:

$$
U_t := \big(\mathsf{D}_{K_t}^{\delta_U,\lambda_U}\big)^3(U^{\text{raw}}_t).
$$

### 4.5 Corner hazard $H_c$ (repulsor on true walls)

From the **true** wall mask $W$ (known to the runner), define

$$
H_c(y,x)=\mathbf{1}\Big(\sum_{(y',x')\in\mathcal{N}(y,x)} \mathbf{1}_W(y',x') \,\ge 2\Big).
$$

This field is static within an episode.

## 5) Potential composition and schema bias

### 5.1 Channel‑agnostic composition

Let the **attractor stack**

$$
A_t=\{G^A_t,\; G^B_t,\; N_t+\gamma_t U_t\},
$$

and the **repulsor stack**

$$
R_t=\{V_t,\; H_c\}\quad(\text{include }H_c\text{ only if enabled}).
$$

Weights (some learned online):

$$
w^{\text{attr}}=(w^A_t,w^B_t,w^{\text{nov}}),\quad
w^{\text{rep}}=(k^{\text{trail}},k^{\text{corner}}).
$$

The **base potential** is the linear superposition

$$
\boxed{\;
P^{\text{base}}_t
= w^A_t G^A_t + w^B_t G^B_t + w^{\text{nov}}\,(N_t+\gamma_t U_t)
 - k^{\text{trail}} V_t - k^{\text{corner}} H_c .
}
$$

The runner sets $\gamma_t\in[0,0.25]$ inversely to trail strength at the current cell; if the trail is strong (oscillation), it suppresses frontier pull.

### 5.2 Schema field (slow bias)

**Features.** Define per‑cell features $f_t(y,x)\in\mathbb{R}^{6}$:

$$
f_t(y,x)=\big(G^A_t,\; G^B_t,\; N_t,\; V_t,\; H_c,\; \|\nabla P^\dagger_t\|\big)(y,x),
$$

where $P^\dagger_t$ is the base potential used **only for feature extraction** (the runner constructs $P^\dagger_t$ with schema disabled), and $\nabla$ is the grid gradient via central finite differences; $\|\cdot\|$ is the Euclidean norm.

**Tiling.** Fix a tile size $q$ and numbers $(n_y,n_x)=\big(\lceil H/q\rceil,\lceil W/q\rceil\big)$. Tile $V$ into (overlapping‑free) blocks

$$
\mathcal{T}_{i,j}=\{(y,x):\; i q\le y<\min((i+1)q,H),\ j q\le x<\min((j+1)q,W)\}.
$$

Pool each tile to a mean feature

$$
x_{i,j}(t)=\frac{1}{|\mathcal{T}_{i,j}|}\sum_{(y,x)\in \mathcal{T}_{i,j}} f_t(y,x)\in\mathbb{R}^{d},\quad d=6.
$$

**Local prototypes.** Each tile $(i,j)$ has $K$ prototype vectors

$$
W_{i,j,k}(t)\in\mathbb{R}^{d},\quad k=1,\dots,K.
$$

Define activations

$$
y_{i,j,k}(t)=\langle W_{i,j,k}(t),\,x_{i,j}(t)\rangle.
$$

**Learning rule.** Maintain a BCM sliding threshold $\theta_{i,j,k}(t)$ (running average of $y^2$) and a previous activation $y^{\text{prev}}_{i,j,k}$. Let $\mathcal{K}_{i,j}(t)$ be the indices of the top‑$\kappa$ activations at tile $(i,j)$ (soft WTA). For winners $k\in\mathcal{K}_{i,j}(t)$:

$$
\begin{aligned}
&\theta_{i,j,k}(t+1)=(1-\tau)\,\theta_{i,j,k}(t)+\tau\,y_{i,j,k}(t)^2,\\
&W_{i,j,k}(t+1) \propto
W_{i,j,k}(t) + \eta\, y_{i,j,k}(t)\,\big(x_{i,j}(t) - y_{i,j,k}(t)\,W_{i,j,k}(t)\big)
 - \lambda_{\text{slow}}\,(y_{i,j,k}(t)-y^{\text{prev}}_{i,j,k})\,W_{i,j,k}(t),
\end{aligned}
$$

followed by normalization $W\leftarrow W/\|W\|$. (The code computes the BCM threshold but gates the update only implicitly via $y$; the formula above matches the implemented Oja term plus the slowness penalty.)

Set $y^{\text{prev}}_{i,j,k}\leftarrow y_{i,j,k}(t)$ for all $k$.

**Schema maps and bias.** Deposit nonnegative activations at tile centers $(c_y,c_x)$ into $K$ maps:

$$
S^{\text{tmp}}_{k}(c_y,c_x)\; \mathrel{+}= \; \max\{0,\,y_{i,j,k}(t)\},
$$

then diffuse each map **without walls**:

$$
S_k(t)=\mathsf{D}^{\delta_S,\lambda_S}_{\varnothing}\big(S^{\text{tmp}}_{k}\big).
$$

The **schema bias** is

$$
B_t=\alpha_{\text{schema}}\sum_{k=1}^K S_k(t)\ \ \ (\ge 0).
$$

### 5.3 Effective potential

$$
\boxed{\quad P^{\text{eff}}_t \;=\; P^{\text{base}}_t \;+\; B_t .\quad}
$$

## 6) Action selection (discrete, with momentum, no‑backtrack, and Gumbel noise)

Let the candidate neighbor set be

$$
\mathcal{A}_t=\{a\in\{0,1,2,3\}:\ (y_t,x_t)+d(a)\in V\setminus W\}.
$$

Define raw scores $s_t(a)=P^{\text{eff}}_t\big((y_t,x_t)+d(a)\big)$.

* **Momentum:** if a previous action $a_{t-1}$ exists, add a small bias $m\ge 0$:

  $$
  s_t(a_{t-1}) \leftarrow s_t(a_{t-1}) + m .
  $$

* **No‑backtrack when stuck:** if enabled, subtract a large penalty $M\gg 1$ from the immediate reverse of $a_{t-1}$.

* **Temperature schedule from trail:** let $v_{\text{here}}=V_t(y_t,x_t)$. If $v_{\text{here}}>2$,

  $$
  \text{temperature } \tau_t=\min\big(0.5+0.5(v_{\text{here}}-2),\,2.0\big),\quad \text{no‑backtrack = true},\quad m=0,
  $$

  else $\tau_t=0,$ no‑backtrack = false, $m=0.05$.

* **Sampling:** if $\tau_t=0$, choose $a_t=\arg\max_{a\in\mathcal{A}_t} s_t(a)$.
  If $\tau_t>0$, draw i.i.d. $g_a\sim \text{Gumbel}(0,\tau_t)$ and pick

  $$
  a_t=\arg\max_{a\in\mathcal{A}_t}\big[s_t(a)+g_a\big].
  $$

## 7) Online valence learning (reward‑modulated)

On **picking** a target at step $t$,

$$
\begin{aligned}
&\text{if A:}\quad w^A_{t+1}=\operatorname{clip}\big(w^A_t+\eta_v\,r_A,\,-C,C\big),\\
&\text{if B:}\quad w^B_{t+1}=\operatorname{clip}\big(w^B_t+\eta_v\,r_B,\,-C,C\big),
\end{aligned}
$$

with clip constant $C>0$ and learning rate $\eta_v>0$. (Other weights are fixed by config unless you choose to adapt them.)

## 8) One complete timestep as an operator and the semigroup view

Let the **agent memory** be

$$
m_t=\big(G^A_t,G^B_t,N_t,V_t,S_t,K_t,\; w^A_t,w^B_t,\; a_{t-1},\;P^{\text{loc}}_{t-1},\;\pi_{t-1},\;\text{schema params}\big).
$$

Given observation $o_t$, define the **internal update operator**

$$
\mathcal{F}:\ (m_t,o_t)\mapsto \big(m_{t}^{+},\;P^{\text{eff}}_t\big),
$$

which deterministically applies §§4–5. (The only stochasticity is later in action sampling if $\tau_t>0$.)

Define the **policy operator**

$$
\Pi:\ (P^{\text{eff}}_t, m_t^+)\mapsto \text{law of }a_t,
$$

produced by §6 (a degenerate distribution if $\tau_t=0$).

Let $\mathcal{T}$ be the environment transition $ (s_t,a_t)\mapsto (s_{t+1},o_{t+1},r_t,\text{done}_t)$ of §1–§3.

Then **one EFI step** is the composition

$$
(s_t,m_t)\;\xrightarrow{o_t}\; (m_t^+,P^{\text{eff}}_t)=\mathcal{F}(m_t,o_t)\;
\xrightarrow{\Pi}\; a_t\;\xrightarrow{\mathcal{T}}\; (s_{t+1},o_{t+1},r_t,\text{done}_t),
$$

with the learning update $m_{t+1}\leftarrow\text{Learn}(m_t^+,r_t,\text{pickup})$ (valences/schema).

Fixing the ablations/config, the set of maps

$$
\{\Phi_t:\ (s_t,m_t)\mapsto (s_{t+1},m_{t+1})\}_{t\in\mathbb{N}}
$$

generated by composing the **local** operators (seed, diffuse, trail, novelty, frontier, schema, compose, sample, env‑step, learn) forms a **semigroup** under composition (associative; generally not invertible because of clamping, diffusion decay, thresholding, and stochastic choices). Reversible variants (e.g., if every sub‑operator were bijective and $\tau_t=0$ with deterministic backtracking disabled) would live inside a **group**, but the implemented agent is intrinsically dissipative and thus semigroup‑native.

## 9) Metrics (for evaluation)

For an episode of length $T$ with rewards $\{r_t\}$ and positions $\{(y_t,x_t)\}$:

* **Return:** $R=\sum_{t=0}^{T-1} r_t$.
* **Efficiency:** $R/T$.
* **Targets collected:** counts of A and B pickups.
* **Gradient–motion alignment:** for timesteps with movement, define

  $$
  \cos_t=\frac{\langle \nabla P^{\text{eff}}_t(y_t,x_t),\, (y_{t+1}-y_t,\,x_{t+1}-x_t)\rangle}
  {\|\nabla P^{\text{eff}}_t(y_t,x_t)\|\,\|(y_{t+1}-y_t,\,x_{t+1}-x_t)\|},
  $$

  and report $\overline{\cos}=\frac{1}{|\{t:\text{moved}\}|}\sum \cos_t$.
* **Valence snapshot:** current $w^A_T,w^B_T,w^{\text{nov}}$.

Across runs, averages and standard deviations over episodes/seeds are computed in the usual way.

## 10) Invariants and basic properties

* **Nonnegativity:** if initialized nonnegative, then $G^A_t,G^B_t,N_t,V_t,U_t\ge 0$ for all $t$ (diffusion is convex averaging plus nonnegative decays; injections and clamps are $\max$/$\min$).
* **Bounds:**

  $$
  0\le N_t\le 1,\quad 0\le V_t\le v_{\max},\quad 0\le U_t\le 1.
  $$

  Consequently,

  $$
  \|P^{\text{eff}}_t\|_\infty \le 
  |w^A_t|\,\|G^A_t\|_\infty + |w^B_t|\,\|G^B_t\|_\infty + |w^{\text{nov}}|(\|N_t\|_\infty+\gamma_t)
  + k^{\text{trail}} v_{\max} + k^{\text{corner}} .
  $$
* **Monotonic knowledge:** $K_t$ and $S_t$ are monotone increasing sets; once a cell is marked wall/seen it stays that way.
* **Diffusion stability:** For any $M$, $\|\mathsf{D}_M^{\delta,\lambda}\|_\infty\le 1$ (convex combination followed by zeroing on walls).

## 12) Unifying both agent classes

Both `ChemotaxisAgentCA` and `FieldController` realize the same formal model above. They differ only in:

* where the observation‑to‑seed mapping lives (direct vs. `ControllerAdapter`), and
* whether potential composition is delegated to a method (`compose_P`) or done explicitly in the runner.

The formal equations (§§3–6) cover both paths.

## 13) What is “cellular automaton” here?

Each fast field update (seed, decay, masked diffusion, clamp, local injection) is **local** (radius‑1 per diffusion step), so the joint state

$$
\Xi_t=\big(G^A_t,G^B_t,N_t,V_t,S_t,K_t\big)
$$

evolves by composition of local, shift‑equivariant maps—precisely CA‑style operators—modulated by the agent’s current position and the observation (both local). The per‑step operator family $\{\Phi_t\}$ therefore forms a **(non‑invertible) semigroup** under composition (dissipative due to decay/clamp), matching the intuition in your earlier question.

---

### (Optional) Compact block form

If you stack all active channels into a vector field $X_t\in\mathbb{R}^{V\times C}$, then a single step can be written as

$$
X_{t+1} = \mathcal{G}_{\Theta}(X_t,\;o_t,\;y_t,x_t)
$$

where $\mathcal{G}_{\Theta}$ is built from the following building blocks, each a local operator:

* masked diffusion $\mathsf{D}_{K_t}^{\cdot,\cdot}$ (linear per channel),
* point‑wise affine maps (decays),
* point‑wise nonlinearities (max/min clamps),
* local seeding from $o_t$,
* schema update producing $B_t$,
* linear readout $P^{\text{base}}_t$, then $P^{\text{eff}}_t=P^{\text{base}}_t+B_t$,
* local action selection on the 4‑neighborhood.

Composition of these blocks is associative and determines the per‑step semigroup element.

## 14) Small implementation notes (to keep math aligned with code)

* The BCM threshold $\theta$ is maintained but not used as an explicit multiplicative gate in the weight update; learning is Oja + slowness on the winners (as formalized above).
* Corner hazard $H_c$ is computed from **true** walls once per episode (runner passes it), not from $K_t$.
* The frontier weight $\gamma_t$ is computed from the current trail value at the agent’s position, $\gamma_t=\max(0,0.25\,(1 - V_t(y_t,x_t)/3))$.
* Temperature $\tau_t$ rises linearly with $V_t(y_t,x_t)$ beyond 2.0, capped at 2.0.
